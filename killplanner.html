Assumptions

  • 1v1, every move fully fresh (×1.05), launch rate 1.0, no items. Aura follows Lucario's percent and the stock difference; rage follows Lucario's percent.
  • Every character is a point. The opponent is launched toward the ledge they're near. Bair, dair, ftilt, dash attack, utilt and Extreme Speed hit them standing on the floor (not crouching). Uair and fair hit them in the air at Lucario's short hop (18.19), full hop (37.62) or double-jump (75.24) height.
  • Short-hop aerials deal ×0.85 damage. In this engine that only lowers the damage added to the opponent's percent, so short hop costs a couple of percent, not 15%.
  • Strongest hitbox of each move: bair 14% sweetspot, uair 11% (frame 10), dair landing hit after the 5% first hit, ftilt launcher after the 4% first hit, dash attack early 10% hitbox, fair 6%, utilt 6% hitbox, Extreme Speed 6% (ground and air versions are identical). The opponent's percent is measured before the move starts.
  • Optimal DI is the opponent's best survival DI: every 5° of stick angle, then 1° around the best.
  • How it's fast: a launch's flight depends only on its knockback, angle, the opponent's gravity and fall speed, and DI. So each opponent's flights are simulated once, the knockback needed to KO is read off them for every position, and the kill percent is solved from the knockback formula. Matches the full engine to within 0.04%.
  • Engine limits: KO is checked until hitstun ends, no character hurtbox or ECB, and moving or transforming stage parts use their default layout.
